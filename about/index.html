The Founder

A Trainer Who Refused to Compromise

With over a decade of experience in the fitness industry, our founder built JL Fitness from a simple frustration: great gear didn't exist.

As a personal trainer, she worked with hundreds of clients and wore every brand on the market. Some performed. Some looked good. None did both reliably, affordably, or sustainably.

JL Fitness started as a passion project — a single legging design made for herself and a few clients. The response was immediate. The brand grew from there, built on word of mouth, real feedback from real athletes, and an uncompromising commitment to quality.

Today, JL Fitness outfits athletes across Canada — from recreational gym-goers to competitive runners. Every product is still designed the same way: on a training floor, with real bodies, under real conditions.

Our Values

What We Stand For

Three principles drive every product decision we make.

01

Performance First

Every design decision starts with function. If it doesn't perform at peak intensity, it doesn't make it to market. Style is built on top — never the other way around.

02

Sustainable by Default

High-quality, sustainably sourced materials aren't a feature — they're the baseline. We use recycled fabrics where possible and are committed to reducing our environmental footprint.

03

Built for Everyone

Athletic apparel too often ignores real bodies. JL Fitness offers size-inclusive sizing across every product, designed to flatter and perform at every size.
